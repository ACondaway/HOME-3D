The Living Index turns a personal homepage into a small world: part portfolio, part archive, and part living self-portrait. It can be explored spatially or read through an accessible content index, in both Chinese and English.

The complete guide—including setup, Content Studio, custom assets, scene placement, content modeling, and deployment—lives in the **[Mintlify documentation](https://docs.acondawayuno.com)**.
